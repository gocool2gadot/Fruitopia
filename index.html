About Fruitopia

Chennai's First Avocado-Centered Street Food Kart

Nestled in the bustling lanes of Ashok Nagar, Fruitopia is redefining quick street bites. We believe fast food doesn't have to mean refined oils, heavy sugars, or preservatives.

From our velvety signature avocado smoothie to hearty multigrain guacamole toasts layered with fresh cottage cheese and warm simmering broccoli soup, every dish is prepared with clean ingredients, conscious hygiene, and uncompromising flavor.

  • ✓ Guilt-Free Indulgence: Powered by heart-healthy monounsaturated fats from real Hass avocados.
  • ✓ Pure & Wholesome: Multigrain artisan breads, house-crafted low-fat paneer, and fresh garden herbs.
  • ✓ Pocket Friendly: Premium wellness café quality brought right onto the street at honest prices.
